# StayYaar

Find your stay, yaar!

StayYaar is a student-first accommodation discovery platform
for finding PGs, hostels, and rental rooms.

## The problem

Finding accommodation near a college often means dealing with
scattered information, unclear pricing, outdated listings, and
uncertainty about what a property actually offers.

StayYaar aims to make that information easier to discover,
understand, and compare.

## Who it is for

- Students looking for accommodation near their college.
- Property owners who want to present their accommodation clearly.
- Working professionals looking for nearby stays in future releases.

## Initial scope

The first version will focus on one college area and a small
set of sample listings.

Users will be able to:

- Browse accommodation listings.
- Filter by rent, accommodation type, and selected amenities.
- View property photos, amenities, location, and pricing details.
- See rent and additional charges separately.
- Find an owner's contact information.

## Future directions

Potential additions include owner-managed listings, a defined
verification process, tenant reviews, property comparisons,
roommate discovery, and AI-assisted search.

These features will be considered after the basic discovery
experience works reliably.

## Project status

Planning stage. The features above describe the intended
product and are not implemented yet.

## Development approach

StayYaar is being developed incrementally, with focused commits,
documented decisions, and validation as features are introduced.
